Memory Boundary

Memory Gate is not legal advice, compliance certification, therapy, mediation, or a production privacy system. It is a prototype decision layer showing what must be enforced before VedaPath remembers.

Enforcement Fields

Consent proof

Who consented, what scope they accepted, and how withdrawal works.

Source proof

Source seed, passage dossier, citation, source family, and review status.

Route proof

Local, review, ledger, or blocked route with clear reason and timestamp.

Deletion proof

Retention, export, deletion, audit history, and privacy classification.
