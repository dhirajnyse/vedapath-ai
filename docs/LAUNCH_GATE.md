# VedaPath Launch Gate Seed

This is the v0.5.9 Launch Gate preview for VedaPath AI.

## Product Question

How do we let people feel the promise of VedaPath without allowing public excitement to outrun source clarity, privacy, review, or non-authority boundaries?

## What It Adds

- A public demo readiness screen.
- Scenario-specific launch decisions.
- Visible release gates for source trail, privacy line, scholar review, and no-go copy.
- Copy-ready launch brief, privacy copy, and no-go gate.
- A public-readiness pulse that distinguishes soft demo, founder share, and pilot room.

## Why It Matters

VedaPath is emotionally resonant. That is powerful, but it also increases risk.

The Launch Gate keeps the product honest:

- It can inspire without claiming spiritual authority.
- It can invite feedback without pretending review is complete.
- It can show calm tools without implying therapy, medical advice, crisis support, or ritual instruction.
- It can use browser-local previews without implying durable account memory.

## Launch Scenarios

### Public Demo

Use when sharing the GitHub Pages prototype broadly.

Decision:

- Soft public demo ready.

Requirements:

- Current release label visible.
- Source-first promise visible.
- Privacy and prototype boundaries visible.
- Scholar Review linked.
- No-go claims blocked.

### Founder Share

Use when writing a personal note to trusted friends, builders, or early supporters.

Decision:

- Share-ready founder note.

Requirements:

- Founder story stays honest.
- Prototype status is explicit.
- Feedback asks for source challenges, not praise.
- No production memory, audio, or scholar-board claim.

### Pilot Room

Use when inviting a small learning group, teacher, cultural reviewer, or pilot cohort.

Decision:

- Pilot invite requires reviewer contact.

Requirements:

- Audience, duration, and feedback route defined.
- Consent copy written.
- Export and deletion path planned.
- Named reviewer attached.
- Durable storage model ready before shared records become production data.

## Launch No-Go Rules

Must not:

- Claim the AI is a guru, priest, therapist, doctor, scholar board, or final authority.
- Imply every answer is verified.
- Imply every source is complete.
- Treat browser-local reflections as production memory.
- Promote audio or chant playback before licensing and reviewer gates.
- Offer ritual instruction, initiation guidance, crisis support, or medical advice.
- Invite group pilots before consent, export, deletion, and reviewer ownership are clear.

## Public Copy Direction

Preferred plain-language positioning:

> VedaPath AI is a source-first calm learning companion for exploring Vedic and Hindu philosophical texts with citations, boundaries, and review gates.

Do not lead with:

- "Ancient AI guru"
- "Spiritual authority"
- "Therapy through scripture"
- "All Vedic knowledge"
- "Scholar-approved answers"
