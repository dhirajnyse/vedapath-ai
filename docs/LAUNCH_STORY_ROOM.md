# VedaPath Launch Story Room

This is the v0.6.4 Launch Story Room for VedaPath AI.

## Purpose

The Launch Story Room helps write public launch copy that is warm, source-first, and bounded. It keeps the product invitation clear without pretending the prototype has verified answers, production storage, licensed audio, a scholar board, or spiritual authority.

## Audience Variants

- Learners who need a calm first doorway into sources.
- Families who want shared reflection without pressure or ritual instruction.
- Builders who need to understand the trust architecture.
- Reviewers who should inspect claims, source categories, feedback flow, and boundaries.

## Required Story Fields

- audience
- tone
- primary_ask
- launch_state
- allowed_claims
- no_go_claims
- feedback_route
- release_id

## Allowed Claims

- VedaPath is a source-first learning companion prototype.
- Serious answers should show source family, citation, and boundary.
- Feedback becomes review work before product behavior changes.
- Calm surfaces are reflection support, not therapy or ritual instruction.

## No-Go Claims

- No verified-answer claim.
- No guru, therapy, ritual, medical, emergency, or crisis-support claim.
- No scholar-board or final-approval claim.
- No production-storage, account-sync, or licensed-audio claim.
- No claim that public feedback changes knowledge automatically.

## Sprint Pause

After v0.6.4, the five-build sprint pauses for founder direction. The next product decision should choose between production implementation, licensed audio planning, and launch refinement.
