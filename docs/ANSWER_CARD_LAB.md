# VedaPath Answer Card Lab

This is the v2.3.8 VedaPath Answer Card Lab release for VedaPath AI.

## Purpose

Answer Card Lab turns retrieval output into a humane answer format.

## Answer Focus

- Sample question: How do I tell short term comfort from a deeper good?
- Source lane: Katha Upanishad 1.2.1
- Boundary: Answer card preview, not public AI response.

## Answer Signals

- Intent: Keep the user question and answer intent visible. Prevents generic advice.
- Source: Keep citation, family, and confidence near the answer. Protects trust.
- Boundary: Show what the answer must not become. Prevents overclaim.
- Feedback: Turn concerns into reviewable local tickets. Keeps learning alive.
