# Passage Review Pack

Passage Review Pack is the first visible review gate between prototype source seeds and source-backed answer authority.

It does not claim final scholarship. It shows the operating discipline VedaPath needs before a passage can power production retrieval.

## Product Role

Passage Review Pack answers one trust question:

Can this source candidate safely support an answer yet?

Each passage dossier shows:

- citation
- source family
- review status
- prototype meaning capsule
- allowed product use
- what the passage must not be used to claim
- missing production fields
- evaluation checks
- review decision

## Why It Matters

Retrieval without review can sound confident too early.

VedaPath should not simply find a likely source and speak. It should ask:

- Is the citation precise?
- Is the source family visible?
- Is this a paraphrase, translation, commentary, or modern analogy?
- Is the boundary clear?
- Is this safe for calm or personal-life use?
- Has a reviewer accepted this record for the intended use?

## Prototype Behavior

The v0.4.0 page lets users:

- search 12 passage dossiers
- filter by review lens
- inspect a selected passage
- see allowed use and restricted use
- mark local decisions such as accepted for prototype, needs scholar, or boundary flagged
- copy a review-card JSON handoff
- open the best current product room for the passage

All decisions are local browser preview state.

## Production Requirements

Before a passage can become authoritative, VedaPath needs:

- verified Sanskrit or source text
- source edition metadata
- translation policy
- translation rights or public-domain strategy
- reviewer identity and decision
- commentary or school notes when needed
- safety boundary for calm, health, relationship, or ritual use
- evaluation checks for citation mismatch and source-family confusion

## Launch Boundary

Passage Review Pack is a review operating model, not a final corpus.

It protects the product from turning beautiful source references into unsupported authority.
