# Calm Circle

Calm Circle is a shared-calm room for VedaPath AI.

The core idea:

Before a group tries to reach a verdict, it can pause around one source candidate, one shared question, one boundary, and one small agreement.

## Why It Matters

Personal calm often breaks down inside shared moments: family decisions, team pressure, friendship repair, and inner conflict.

Calm Circle makes shared steadiness practical without pretending VedaPath is a mediator, therapist, relationship counselor, workplace advisor, legal advisor, ritual authority, or judge.

## Current Prototype

The v0.4.7 Calm Circle includes:

- four circle types
- source card for each circle
- shared question
- small agreement
- two reflection inputs
- local saved circle notes
- copyable circle brief
- next-room routing
- privacy and consent boundary

All saved state is browser-local only.

## Circle Types

- Family table
- Team pressure
- Friendship repair
- Inner circle

## Product Rule

Calm Circle should never decide for the group.

It should:

- slow the moment down
- make one source candidate visible
- ask one shared question
- state what the app is not doing
- suggest one small agreement
- route to the next useful VedaPath room

## Consent Boundary

Shared records require higher care than private notes.

Production should require:

- consent from all participants
- deletion controls
- export controls
- safety review for unsafe-contact scenarios
- no hidden profiling of relationships
- clear source and policy governance
- reviewed shared-record schema
