# VedaPath Reviewer Identity and Access Gate

This is the v0.6.2 Reviewer Identity and Access Gate for VedaPath AI.

The purpose is to make review authority scoped, auditable, and respectful of privacy.

## Access Promise

No source, answer, audio, or public launch claim should be promoted because an unnamed or all-powerful reviewer approved it.

Each reviewer decision must show:

- reviewer role
- permission scope
- decision state
- evidence note
- display policy
- audit event
- release id

## Reviewer Roles

### Sanskrit reviewer

Can review:

- Sanskrit text presence
- transliteration quality
- word lens caution
- meter or chandas status
- edition evidence

Cannot approve:

- product safety
- rights and licensing
- launch copy
- final spiritual authority

### Translation reviewer

Can review:

- plain meaning clarity
- edition context
- rendering notes
- interpretation caveats
- compare-view prompts

Cannot approve:

- universal doctrinal conclusions
- ritual instruction
- product safety
- media rights

### Boundary reviewer

Can review:

- guru-voice risk
- therapy or medical overreach
- ritual authority language
- crisis-use boundaries
- modern proof claims
- public demo no-go copy

Cannot approve:

- Sanskrit accuracy alone
- translation quality alone
- asset rights
- scholar identity verification

### Rights reviewer

Can review:

- audio permission
- translation permission
- image and asset permission
- attribution
- takedown path
- allowed product use

Cannot approve:

- text meaning
- Sanskrit accuracy
- spiritual authority
- product boundary language

## Display Policy

VedaPath should show public role labels before exposing personal identity.

User-facing labels can say:

- Sanskrit reviewed
- Translation reviewed
- Boundary reviewed
- Rights held
- Review required

Private fields should stay protected:

- reviewer legal name
- contact details
- affiliation details
- contract notes
- full internal evidence notes

## Permission Rules

- One role approves one lane.
- Production approval requires a private verified reviewer id.
- Every approval, hold, block, rollback, or annotation creates an audit event.
- A public label must not expose private reviewer identity by default.
- The AI cannot certify its own source or boundary decision.

## No-Go Checks

- no anonymous production approval
- no merged reviewer roles
- no public identity exposure by default
- no AI self-certification
- no audio unlock without rights review
- no final spiritual authority claim

## Product Boundary

This gate is a product plan, not a live identity system.

It does not verify scholars, expose reviewer identities, create legal compliance, or grant production authority by itself.

## v0.6.3 Direction

The next release should add a Public Feedback Intake so launch visitors can report source issues, category confusion, UX friction, and boundary concerns as review tickets rather than free-form praise.
