# Before Reply

Before Reply is a source-backed calm communication prototype.

It is designed for moments when a user is about to send a reactive message and wants to pause, name the situation, and draft a cleaner reply.

## Product Shape

- Pick the reply state: heated, hurt, defensive, or boundary.
- Name what happened in plain language.
- Choose a reply tone: warm boundary, clear ask, or repair.
- See one source candidate, text family, reply question, and boundary.
- Draft a calmer reply with one concrete next step.
- Copy the reply or save a device-local draft.

## Boundary

Before Reply is communication support. It is not therapy, legal advice, emergency support, conflict mediation, or a recommendation to remain in unsafe communication.

## Why It Matters

Many people lose calm in the small moment before they press send. VedaPath can help by preserving agency, source context, and restraint at the exact point where a calmer action matters.
