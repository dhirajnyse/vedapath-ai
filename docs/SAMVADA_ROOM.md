# Samvada Room

Samvada Room is a source-backed respectful dialogue prototype.

It is designed for moments when two people disagree and need a calmer shared question before the next reply, decision, or argument.

## Product Shape

- Pick the disagreement shape: facts, meaning, values, or action.
- State both sides fairly.
- Choose an intent: learn, clarify, or decide.
- See one source candidate, text family, pramana level, and boundary.
- Generate a shared question instead of a verdict.
- Copy or save a device-local samvada map.

## Boundary

Samvada Room is reflection support. It is not therapy, legal advice, mediation, a verdict engine, or a recommendation to continue unsafe conflict.

## Why It Matters

VedaPath should not only help a person feel calm alone. It should help people speak with precision and respect when they differ. Samvada Room makes disagreement more source-aware without pretending all views are identical.
