# VedaPath Review Intake Desk

This is the v2.0.5 VedaPath Review Intake Desk release for VedaPath AI.

## Purpose

Review Intake Desk creates the first visible bridge between guided reading and human review.

## Source Candidate

- Source: Bhagavad Gita 2.48
- Text family: Bhagavad Gita | Smriti
- Boundary: Review intake prototype, not submitted workflow.

## Review Signals

- Queue cards: Every trace becomes a scoped review item. Makes review concrete.
- Severity label: Medium and high risks are visible. Helps prioritize.
- No-go line: Misuse stays attached to the item. Prevents context loss.
- Prototype boundary: The desk does not claim real approval. Keeps scope honest.
