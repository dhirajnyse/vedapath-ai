# VedaPath Mobile Reader Polish

This is the v2.0.3 VedaPath Mobile Reader Polish release for VedaPath AI.

## Purpose

Mobile Reader Polish keeps VedaPath's most personal reading flow clean on small screens.

## Source Candidate

- Source: Isha Upanishad 1
- Text family: Upanishad | Shruti
- Boundary: Responsive reader polish, not native app release.

## Reader Signals

- Single column: The reader stacks without horizontal overflow. Keeps mobile calm.
- Stable actions: Save, copy, and clear controls remain obvious. Prevents frustration.
- Readable layers: Meaning, boundary, reflection, and carry cards are scannable. Protects depth.
- No crowding: The logo and cards support focus rather than decoration. Keeps attention steady.

## Founder Action

Protect the simplicity promise on mobile before the reader becomes more powerful.
