# VedaPath AI Brand System

## Brand Essence

VedaPath AI is warm, luminous, grounded, and precise.

It should feel like ancient knowledge made accessible through a clean modern interface.

## Name

VedaPath AI

Meaning:

- Veda: knowledge, sacred textual tradition
- Path: guided learning journey
- AI: assistant technology, not authority

## Tagline Options

1. Understand sacred texts through source, context, and commentary.
2. Walk the source. Understand the wisdom.
3. Source-first guidance through Vedic knowledge.
4. Ancient texts. Clear paths.
5. Ask with curiosity. Learn with sources.

Recommended tagline:

Understand sacred texts through source, context, and commentary.

## Brand Voice

Tone:

- calm
- respectful
- precise
- gently corrective
- never preachy
- never mystical about the AI itself

Sample phrase:

"Let us look at the source first."

## Color Palette

Bhagwa is the spiritual anchor, but the UI should not become visually loud.

Primary:

- Bhagwa: `#D65A1F`
- Deep Ochre: `#A83E12`
- Sacred Gold: `#E0A83B`

Base:

- Warm Ivory: `#FFF7EA`
- Palm Leaf: `#E9D8B8`
- Soft Sand: `#F4E6CF`

Text:

- Ink: `#1F1A17`
- Muted Brown: `#5B4638`

Accent:

- Peacock Green: `#145C4A`
- Deep Indigo: `#29335C`

Usage:

- Bhagwa for primary actions, active states, key highlights, and logo energy.
- Ivory for reading surfaces.
- Ink for primary text.
- Peacock green or indigo for secondary trust and knowledge accents.

## Visual Identity

Logo direction:

- 3D luminous Bhagwa flame/path symbol
- inspired by a rising path, folded manuscript leaf, and subtle diya flame
- no deity figure
- no AI robot face
- no over-complex temple iconography
- no political symbolism
- no heavy gradients in the UI, though the logo can have depth and light

## Logo Prompt Direction

Generate a 3D logo mark, not a wordmark.

Concept:

A luminous saffron-orange path forming a subtle V shape, rising into a small flame-like point, with hints of palm-leaf manuscript geometry. Premium, modern, spiritual, energetic, clean, and vector-friendly. No text, no deity, no human figure, no temple, no political symbols, no watermark.

## Typography Direction

Use simple, highly readable fonts.

Recommended app pairing:

- Interface: Inter, IBM Plex Sans, or system sans
- Sanskrit/display accents: Noto Serif Devanagari or Noto Serif

Typography rule:

Never sacrifice reading comfort for atmosphere.

## Brand Guardrails

Avoid:

- fake ancient parchment everywhere
- overloaded mandala backgrounds
- neon saffron
- AI robot imagery
- divine/personified AI imagery
- guru-like avatar
- aggressive spiritual claims

Use:

- quiet surfaces
- source cards
- clear labels
- restrained Bhagwa highlights
- deep reading comfort
