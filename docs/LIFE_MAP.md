# Life Map

Life Map is a personal-life calm router for VedaPath AI.

The core idea:

Choose the real-life moment first. Then receive one source candidate, one boundary, and one next action.

## Why It Matters

Many users will not arrive with a clean scripture question. They will arrive with pressure from work, family, money, health uncertainty, digital noise, or sleep drift.

Life Map makes VedaPath useful in that moment without pretending the AI is a therapist, financial advisor, doctor, lawyer, relationship counselor, ritual authority, or guru.

## Current Prototype

The v0.4.6 Life Map includes:

- six real-life domains
- one source candidate per domain
- source family and pramana label
- clear boundary for each domain
- one reflection prompt
- one carry action
- next-room routing
- browser-local saved maps
- seven-day local pattern preview
- copyable handoff

All saved state is browser-local only.

## Domains

- Work pressure
- Family friction
- Money worry
- Health uncertainty
- Digital noise
- Sleep drift

## Product Rule

Life Map should never solve the whole life problem.

It should:

- slow the moment down
- name the source candidate
- state what the app is not doing
- offer one small action
- route to the next VedaPath room

## Privacy Boundary

Life Map memory is a prototype only.

Production should require:

- explicit accounts
- user consent
- export controls
- deletion controls
- reviewed source data
- safety review for personal distress flows
- no hidden profiling or scoring
