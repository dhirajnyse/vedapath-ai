Source-first product plan

Blueprint for a trusted Vedic learning companion.

VedaPath AI should help users move from curiosity to understanding through source-cited answers, careful categories, progressive depth, and reviewed correction loops.

Open Prototype Raw Markdown
Positioning

What It Is

  • A study assistant for Vedic and Hindu philosophical texts.
  • A citation-first scripture explorer.
  • A Sanskrit and concept explainer.
  • A bridge between modern questions and traditional sources.
Guardrails

What It Is Not

  • Not a digital guru, oracle, fortune teller, or ritual authority.
  • Not a replacement for teachers, priests, scholars, or tradition.
  • Not a tool for inflated claims like "the Vedas prove all modern science."

Source Labels

VedaPath can cover a broad Hindu wisdom landscape, but every answer must label where it is standing.

Signature Features

Pramana Meter

Labels whether an answer is direct source, commentary, scholarly view, analogy, or speculation.

Mantra Lens

Future verse-level view with Sanskrit, transliteration, word meanings, meter, and recitation context.

Claim Checker

Separates what the source says from tradition, modern claims, and overextended interpretations.

Samvada Mode

Compares interpretations respectfully without pretending every school says the same thing.

Learning Path

Adapts to beginners, Sanskrit learners, philosophy readers, and teachers.

Calm Path

Guides source-backed reflection for personal steadiness without becoming therapy, ritual advice, or a guru voice.

Correction Loop

User suggestions become reviewed annotations, not silent knowledge-base rewrites.
